Method

  
Inputs are CME's daily settlements for Three-Month (SR3) and One-Month (SR1) SOFR futures, plus the New York Fed's published SOFR for days already elapsed. SR1 settles on the average of SOFR over a calendar month; SR3 on SOFR compounded from the third Wednesday of its named month to the third Wednesday three months later.

  
Through the last scheduled FOMC meeting, overnight SOFR is held constant between policy decisions, so moves land on the day after each meeting. Further out the curve has one level per month. Levels are fitted by least squares across every quarterly SR3 contract and each serial SR3 and SR1 contract with open interest, with a light smoothness penalty in the back.

  
Futures rates run above true forward rates because futures are settled daily. Each contract is adjusted with a Hull-White one-factor model before fitting; the dashed line shows the curve without that adjustment. The adjustment is a modelling choice and dominates uncertainty past year five.

  
Contracts beyond about seven years carry little or no open interest, and months after the last listed contract hold its level flat. Treat the final years as indicative. The 1M forward is compounded SOFR over each month: a close proxy for, but not the same thing as, CME 1M Term SOFR.
